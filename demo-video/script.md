## Scenes

| # | Scene | On-screen action | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Login screen. Floating balls drift in the background; the big "Let's Sport!" logo bounces gently. Slow cursor drift across the page. | Ever booked a badminton court, and ended up one player short? Or posted in three group chats, and got two maybes and a thumbs up? |
| 2 | Meet Let's Sport | Cursor clicks **Sign up**, types "Sam Rivera", an email and a password, clicks **Create my account**. Confetti bursts and the welcome toast appears. | Meet Let's Sport! It brings your neighbours together for badminton and basketball. Signing up takes ten seconds… and yes, there's confetti. |
| 3 | Find a game | The games board appears with four colourful cards. Cursor hovers a card (it lifts), then clicks the **Basketball** filter, then **All sports** again. | Here's what's happening near you. Every card shows the date, the venue, the skill level, and how many spots are left. Only into basketball? One tap. |
| 4 | Host a game | Clicks **Host a game**. Picks **Badminton**, types "Saturday Smash & Brunch", sets date and time, types the venue, pastes a Google Maps link. The map preview drops a pin. | Want to start your own game? Pick a sport, give it a name, set the time, and drop a Google Maps pin, so nobody ends up at the wrong court. |
| 5 | It's live | Clicks **Post my game**. Confetti; the new card slides into the board with the "Hosting" badge. | Hit post, and your game is live for the whole neighbourhood. |
| 6 | Join in | Scrolls to Priya's "Friday Night Doubles". Clicks **I'm in!** Confetti from the button; the spots bar fills to 4/4 and shows "Full house!". | Spotted a game you like? Tap "I'm in", and you're on the team. Plans change? One tap frees your spot for someone else. |
| 7 | Get there | On Marcus's basketball card, clicks **Show map**. The embedded Google map opens; cursor hovers **Directions**. | On game day, open the map, and get directions right to the court. |
| 8 | Call to action | Clicks **My games**, showing Sam's two games. Slow fade to the end card: "Let's Sport!" logo and "Find your crew. Book the court. Play together!" | Badminton, basketball, and good company, all in one place. Sign up today, host your first game this week… and let's sport! |

## Notes

- Each scene's video is timed to its measured voiceover clip, plus a short pause of about 0.6 s between scenes.
- Subtitles: max 2 lines, about 42 characters per line, split at natural phrases.
- The Google Maps link in scene 4 is a full Google Maps URL for the venue, so the pin shows instantly with no short-link lookup.
